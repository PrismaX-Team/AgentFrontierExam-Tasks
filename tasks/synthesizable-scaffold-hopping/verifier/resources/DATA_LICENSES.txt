ERK2 reference structure data

Numerical structure data are derived directly from PDB archive entry 4O6E.
Depositors: J. Yin and W. Wang.
Structure DOI: https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb4o6e/pdb
Associated primary publication: Blake et al.,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2014.04.068
Archive: https://files.rcsb.org/download/4O6E.cif

PDB archive numerical data are available under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Policy: https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies

The reference SDF reconstructs the deposited 2SH component bonds and absolute
stereochemistry and uses its deposited heavy-atom coordinates. The protein
PDB retains model 1, chain A and observed heavy atoms after removal of ligands,
waters, hydrogens and alternate conformations. Missing atoms are not completed;
protein protonation, charges and scoring preparation are not implied.
Trailing horizontal line padding is removed without changing internal PDB/SDF
columns or deposited scientific coordinates.

SMDD-Bench inspired instance selection. Its dataset card declares Apache-2.0,
but no SMDD YAML, coordinates, witness, code or score-cache payload is copied
into this reference-data subset. Selection attribution:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/extremelyconfused/smdd-bench/tree/77a71c968917c5dbd4c70b143e96b2753dde35c4

These numerical data and computed structures are not experimental evidence
that a submitted candidate binds or has been synthesized.

Prepared scoring receptor

The statements above about an unprepared heavy-only protein apply specifically
to input/structures/erk2_protein_unprepared.pdb. The separate erk2_receptor.pdb
and erk2_receptor.pdbqt are computed preparation products of the same public
4O6E protein, with fixed hydrogens, atom types and charges. They are not a new
experimental structure. Identical prepared coordinates are supplied as visible
scientific input and as trusted verifier resources. No private HER2/10e project
or witness ligand/pose is included.

No patent PDF, image, source prose or PubChem response payload is distributed.

and reaction_templates.json under these trusted verifier resources; visible

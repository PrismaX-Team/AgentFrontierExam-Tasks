Changes in this task: extract the released reactant and product geometries;
retain transition-state geometry and all three electronic energies for training;
retain their atom order and official train/val/test membership; replace original
record identifiers with task identifiers; derive the forward electronic barrier
from released transition-state energy minus reactant energy; separate validation
and test labels and transition-state structures from agent-visible inputs.
No geometry relaxation, energy recalculation or numerical clipping is applied.

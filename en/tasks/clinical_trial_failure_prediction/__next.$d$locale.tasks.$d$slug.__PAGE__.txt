4:T578,The data come from TrialBench and the ClinicalTrials.gov registry. Public input is under /input, and /input/README.md documents every file and column. For each failure mode m in enrollment, safety and efficacy, /input/m/train.csv.gz holds labelled training trials: label 1 means the trial is a success and label 0 that it failed in that mode, following TrialBench's four failure-reason categories (successful trials, poor enrollment, safety and lack of efficacy). The three train files share the same successful trials, under the same trial_id, and differ in their failed trials; they have 13,771, 9,055 and 9,715 rows. /input/test.csv.gz holds the 4,120 test trials without labels. Every test trial belongs to the test set of one or more modes: a successful trial to all three, a failed trial only to the mode it failed in. Which modes a test trial belongs to is not given. Each row is one protocol: free text (title, summary, description, eligibility criteria with parsed inclusion and exclusion forms, primary outcome, dosage, study-design description), structured design and oversight fields, anticipated enrollment where the registry still lists it, start date, site countries, disease ICD-10 codes and MeSH terms, and intervention names, types, MeSH terms and SMILES strings. Files are gzip-compressed UTF-8 CSV keyed by an opaque trial_id, and registry identifiers inside the text are removed.f:X

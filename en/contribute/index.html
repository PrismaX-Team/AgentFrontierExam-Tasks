Research contributions

Contribute a research task to Agent Frontier Exam

This call focuses on L1 research challenges. Define one scientific goal that existing methods have yet to reach, and the evidence that would demonstrate a breakthrough.

On the Duanyan ReX page, choose “提交科学问题” (Submit a scientific question). Upload one ZIP per task.

Submit a task package on DuanyanContributor guide and template (Chinese) ↗

Contribution policy

01

Co-authorship

Participants become co-authors when three of their tasks are accepted.

02

Area chairs

Area chairs recruit at least five participants, help define scientific domains and research processes, and review tasks at project milestones.

Completing the Duanyan form

  1. 01

    Describe the question

    Give your team, research question, prior work, agent inputs, expected outputs, resources and success criteria. Select “学术合作” (academic collaboration). The default level is L1. If appropriate, choose L2 or “暂不确定” (undecided).

  2. 02

    Attach the task package

    Place task_package/ at the ZIP root alongside any necessary 补充材料/ (supplementary materials). If materials are missing, select “部分材料待补充” or “需协助构建任务” and describe the gap or help needed.

  3. 03

    Confirm permissions and submit

    Indicate task use, training or capability improvement, and public release separately. Retain a copy. For revisions, give the coordinating team the task name and version.

AFE Research

Preparing your research task

Choosing a question

01

Establish the frontier

Cite related work and date your assessment of what remains out of reach.

02

Identify the research judgment

Describe the inference, design, experiment or proof the agent must carry out.

03

Bound the task

Set the inputs, tools, resources, deliverables and permitted scope of exploration.

04

Define success in advance

Specify the evidence and controls required for the claim. A negative result can meet the goal when its sufficiency conditions are defined in advance.

Harbor task package

Package specification→

task_package/

  1. task.toml

    Runtime settings and AFE metadata.

  2. instruction.md

    The research question, deliverables and acceptance criteria the agent reads.

  3. environment/

    Build the agent’s workspace and provide the inputs and tools it may use.

  4. tests/

    Build an independent verifier and score the submitted artifacts.

  5. solution/

    Optional reference methods, one directory per method.
